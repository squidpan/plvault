# Genesis Reveal Storyboard

## Purpose

Identify how the Genesis 1-11 and Genesis 12-50 overview videos progressively construct the completed one-page synthesis. This is a method-analysis artifact, not a retelling of Genesis.

## Governing observation

The presentation does not reveal the poster as a collection of equal facts. It first supplies an orientation, establishes an intended world, introduces a governing tension, accumulates evidence into a recurring pattern, poses an unresolved question, reveals a hinge response, tests that response through repeated failure, and ends by reconnecting the final outcome to the opening purpose.

## Macrostructure

| Act | Content scope | Narrative function | Requirements analogue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orientation | Genesis as two parts joined by a hinge | Give the audience a map before detail | Initiative scope and transformation thesis |
| Act I | Genesis 1-11: God and the world | Establish intended state, breakdown, and systemic consequences | Current operating model, root problem, and business impact |
| Hinge | Genesis 12:1-3 | Answer the question created by Act I | Strategic decision or pivotal capability |
| Act II | Genesis 12-50: Abraham's family | Test whether the response survives implementation risk and repeated failure | Transformation workstreams, controls, exceptions, and delivery risk |
| Resolution | Joseph and Judah | Reinterpret the whole story and reconnect it to the original purpose | Evidence of value and target-state outcome |
| Open dependency | Family in Egypt; promises incomplete | Provide conceptual closure while preserving forward work | Deferred scope, roadmap, and next initiative |

## Visual grammar extracted from Genesis

1. **Orient before explaining.** Reveal the macrostructure before local detail.
2. **Begin with intended purpose.** A problem has meaning only relative to the world or outcome it disrupts.
3. **Show causality spatially.** Place consequences near their causes before introducing cross-story connections.
4. **Accumulate evidence before naming the pattern.** Let viewers see repetition, then provide the analytical synthesis.
5. **Use a hinge, not a generic midpoint.** The center must answer the question created by the first act and govern the second.
6. **Declare interpretive themes.** Tell viewers what repeated risk and preserved objective to watch across later workstreams.
7. **Repeat with variation.** Similar structures across episodes make systemic patterns visible without treating events as identical.
8. **Converge before concluding.** The final case should bring several earlier themes together.
9. **Make the ending reach backward.** Resolution should reconnect to the opening purpose, not merely describe the last event.
10. **Allow an open ending.** A one-pager can show achieved understanding while identifying remaining dependencies.

## PoC implications

The Farm Credit PoC should begin with one bounded feature and a small number of user stories and acceptance criteria. The first test is not whether every artifact fits. It is whether a stakeholder can follow a coherent path from business purpose through problem evidence and hinge capability to a validated outcome, while navigating to supporting artifacts through stable references.

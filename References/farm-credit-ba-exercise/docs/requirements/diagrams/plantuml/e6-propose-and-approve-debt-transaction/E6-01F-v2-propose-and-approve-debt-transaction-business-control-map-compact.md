# E6-01F-v2-propose-and-approve-debt-transaction-business-control-map-compact

## Business Question

What are the four primary control gates in the approval workflow?

## Purpose

Provide a compact presentation-friendly version of the detailed control map.

## How to Explain It

This is the compact version of the control map. It communicates the same four control gates—data quality, submission integrity, approval governance, and audit integrity—but with less detail. I would use this version in an interview or presentation when I need to explain the control framework quickly, then refer to the detailed version for requirements workshops or audit discussions.

## Key Talking Points

- Four controls protect the transaction from draft through approval.
- The compact view is optimized for fast explanation.
- It preserves the relationship between control points and lifecycle stages.
- The detailed and compact diagrams are complementary rather than competing versions.

## What This Diagram Does Not Show

The detailed validation items, authority checks, or audit evidence listed in the detailed version.

# US-E6-503 Deliver Compliance and Regulatory Reports

## TL;DR

Deliver approved post-trade reports to designated compliance and regulatory recipients through controlled and traceable channels.

## User Story

As a Compliance or Regulatory Reporting Analyst,  
I want to deliver approved post-trade reports to designated recipients,  
so that required reporting obligations are completed accurately and on time.

## Business Value

This story supports:

- Compliance with reporting obligations
- Timely delivery
- Controlled external distribution
- Evidence of submission
- Operational accountability
- Audit traceability

## Scope

This story includes:

- Selecting an approved report version
- Identifying the applicable reporting obligation
- Confirming the required recipient and delivery deadline
- Validating recipient and delivery-channel information
- Delivering the report through an approved channel
- Recording delivery date, time, recipient, report version, and outcome
- Preserving submission acknowledgements or reference identifiers
- Recording failed or rejected delivery attempts

This story excludes:

- Generating the report
- Internal report publication
- Resolving reporting discrepancies
- Interpreting regulatory requirements
- Correcting rejected report content
- Archiving the final reporting package

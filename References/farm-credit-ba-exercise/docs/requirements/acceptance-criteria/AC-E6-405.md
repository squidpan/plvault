# AC-E6-405 Record Settlement Completion

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-405.1 Final Settlement Status Required

Given all settlement instructions for the issuance have reached a final status,

When the Settlement Operations Analyst begins settlement-completion verification,

Then the system displays the final status of each instruction.

---

### AC-405.2 Securities Movement Verification

Given settlement-completion verification is in progress,

When the analyst reviews the issuance,

Then the system confirms that all required securities movements have completed successfully.

---

### AC-405.3 Cash Movement Verification

Given settlement-completion verification is in progress,

When the analyst reviews the issuance,

Then the system confirms that all required cash movements have completed successfully.

---

### AC-405.4 Dealer Transaction Completion

Given multiple dealer transactions are associated with the issuance,

When settlement completion is evaluated,

Then every required dealer transaction must be in a settled status.

---

### AC-405.5 Blocking Exception Prevention

Given one or more blocking settlement exceptions remain unresolved,

When the analyst attempts to mark the issuance settled,

Then the system prevents settlement completion,

And identifies the unresolved blocking exceptions.

---

### AC-405.6 Final Settled Quantities and Amounts

Given all settlement activity has completed,

When the final settlement record is created,

Then the system records the settled quantities, principal amounts, accrued interest when applicable, and total cash amounts.

---

### AC-405.7 Settlement Date and Time

Given settlement completion is recorded,

When the issuance is marked settled,

Then the system preserves the settlement date and time.

---

### AC-405.8 Settlement Completion Actor

Given settlement completion is recorded,

When the issuance is marked settled,

Then the system preserves the user or system actor responsible for recording completion.

---

### AC-405.9 Successful Issuance Settlement

Given all required securities and cash movements have completed and no blocking exceptions remain,

When the analyst confirms settlement completion,

Then the system marks the issuance as settled.

---

### AC-405.10 Settlement Evidence Preservation

Given an issuance has been marked settled,

When the final settlement record is saved,

Then the system preserves supporting settlement statuses, acknowledgements, exception history, and completion evidence.

---

### AC-405.11 Downstream Availability

Given the issuance has been marked settled,

When settlement completion processing finishes,

Then the finalized settlement information is available to downstream accounting, reconciliation, reporting, and lifecycle processes.

---

### AC-405.12 Duplicate Completion Prevention

Given the issuance is already marked settled,

When another settlement-completion attempt is made,

Then the system prevents creation of an unintended duplicate completion record.

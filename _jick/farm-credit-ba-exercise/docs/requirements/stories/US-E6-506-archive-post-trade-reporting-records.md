# US-E6-506 Archive Post-Trade Reporting Records

## TL;DR

Archive finalized post-trade reports, delivery evidence, reconciliation results, and discrepancy records in a complete and retrievable reporting package.

## User Story

As a Records or Reporting Analyst,  
I want to archive finalized post-trade reporting records,  
so that complete reporting evidence remains protected, retrievable, and available for audit or historical review.

## Business Value

This story supports:

- Records retention
- Regulatory and audit readiness
- Historical traceability
- Controlled access to finalized records
- Protection from unauthorized changes
- Efficient record retrieval

## Scope

This story includes:

- Selecting finalized reporting records eligible for archival
- Assembling the report and related evidence
- Including publication and delivery history
- Including reconciliation and discrepancy-resolution records
- Applying the required retention classification
- Recording archive metadata
- Protecting archived records from unauthorized modification
- Making archived records retrievable by authorized users

This story excludes:

- Generating or correcting reports
- Publishing or delivering reports
- Resolving open discrepancies
- Changing retention policies
- Permanently deleting archived records
- Defining storage-platform implementation details

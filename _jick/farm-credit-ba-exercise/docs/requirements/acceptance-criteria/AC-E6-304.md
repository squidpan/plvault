# AC-E6-304 Record Execution Results

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-304.1 Completed Allocation Required

Given the issuance has been executed,

And allocation processing has completed,

When execution-result recording begins,

Then the completed execution and allocation data are available for finalization.

---

### AC-304.2 Execution Summary Recorded

Given the execution results are ready for finalization,

When the results are recorded,

Then the executed issuance amount, execution date and time, and issuance identifier are preserved.

---

### AC-304.3 Allocation Summary Recorded

Given dealer allocations have been completed,

When the execution results are recorded,

Then the dealer-level allocations, total allocated quantity, and any unallocated quantity are preserved.

---

### AC-304.4 Source Traceability

Given the final execution record has been created,

When the record is reviewed,

Then it is traceable to the booked issuance, execution event, accepted dealer orders, and allocation results.

---

### AC-304.5 Settlement Readiness

Given the execution and allocation results have been recorded successfully,

When final validation completes,

Then the issuance is marked ready for settlement processing.

---

### AC-304.6 Incomplete Result Prevention

Given required execution or allocation information is missing,

When finalization is requested,

Then the system prevents the issuance from being marked ready for settlement,

And records the validation reason.

---

### AC-304.7 Finalization Audit

Given the execution results have been finalized,

When the final record is stored,

Then the finalization date, time, and responsible user or process are preserved.

---

### AC-304.8 Final Record Protection

Given the issuance has been marked ready for settlement,

When a change to finalized execution results is requested,

Then the system prevents an unauthorized direct change,

And requires the change to follow the authorized correction process.

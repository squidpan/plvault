# AC-E6-505 Resolve and Track Reporting Discrepancies

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-E6-505-01 — Authorize Discrepancy Management

**Given** a user requests access to a reporting discrepancy,  
**When** the request is evaluated,  
**Then** the system must verify that the user is authorized to review or manage the discrepancy.

### AC-E6-505-02 — Create a Traceable Discrepancy Record

**Given** reconciliation identifies a discrepancy,  
**When** the discrepancy is recorded,  
**Then** the system must assign a unique identifier and associate it with the affected report version and source records.

### AC-E6-505-03 — Record Discrepancy Details

**Given** a discrepancy record is created,  
**When** its details are stored,  
**Then** the system must retain the discrepancy type, affected field or record, expected value, reported value, tolerance, and date identified.

### AC-E6-505-04 — Assign Ownership

**Given** an unresolved discrepancy exists,  
**When** investigation begins,  
**Then** the system must assign the discrepancy to an authorized owner or responsible group.

### AC-E6-505-05 — Track Discrepancy Status

**Given** a discrepancy is under investigation,  
**When** its state changes,  
**Then** the system must record an approved status such as open, assigned, under investigation, pending approval, resolved, rejected, or reopened.

### AC-E6-505-06 — Record Investigation Activity

**Given** an analyst investigates a discrepancy,  
**When** investigation activity occurs,  
**Then** the system must retain relevant notes, findings, supporting evidence, user, date, and time.

### AC-E6-505-07 — Identify the Root Cause

**Given** sufficient investigation has been completed,  
**When** a cause is determined,  
**Then** the system must record the identified root cause or document that the cause remains undetermined.

### AC-E6-505-08 — Identify the Corrective Action

**Given** a discrepancy requires correction,  
**When** a proposed resolution is recorded,  
**Then** the system must identify whether the corrective action applies to the report, source record, mapping, calculation, process, or approved tolerance.

### AC-E6-505-09 — Prevent Unauthorized Direct Changes

**Given** a discrepancy identifies an incorrect authoritative source record,  
**When** the discrepancy is managed,  
**Then** the system must not permit an unauthorized user to modify the source record through the discrepancy workflow.

### AC-E6-505-10 — Require Resolution Evidence

**Given** a discrepancy is proposed for resolution,  
**When** the resolution is submitted for approval,  
**Then** the system must require sufficient notes or supporting evidence to explain the corrective action and resulting outcome.

### AC-E6-505-11 — Require Authorized Resolution Approval

**Given** a proposed resolution is ready for decision,  
**When** it is approved or rejected,  
**Then** the system must verify that the decision-maker is authorized to perform that action.

### AC-E6-505-12 — Record Resolution Approval

**Given** an authorized user approves a proposed resolution,  
**When** the approval is recorded,  
**Then** the system must retain the approver, date and time, resolution details, and supporting evidence.

### AC-E6-505-13 — Record Resolution Rejection

**Given** an authorized user rejects a proposed resolution,  
**When** the rejection is recorded,  
**Then** the system must retain the rejecting user, date and time, rejection reason, and resulting discrepancy status.

### AC-E6-505-14 — Close a Resolved Discrepancy

**Given** a resolution has been approved and required corrective actions are complete,  
**When** closure is requested,  
**Then** the system must permit the discrepancy to be marked resolved or closed.

### AC-E6-505-15 — Prevent Premature Closure

**Given** required corrective actions or approvals remain incomplete,  
**When** closure is requested,  
**Then** the system must prevent the discrepancy from being marked resolved or closed.

### AC-E6-505-16 — Reopen a Discrepancy

**Given** a previously resolved discrepancy is later found to remain incorrect or incomplete,  
**When** an authorized user reopens it,  
**Then** the system must preserve the prior resolution and record the reason, user, date, and time of reopening.

### AC-E6-505-17 — Display Current Resolution Status

**Given** a discrepancy record exists,  
**When** an authorized user reviews it,  
**Then** the system must display its current owner, status, age, investigation history, proposed resolution, and approval outcome.

### AC-E6-505-18 — Preserve Complete History

**Given** a discrepancy changes ownership, status, findings, resolution, approval, or closure state,  
**When** the change is recorded,  
**Then** the system must preserve the previous values and append the new event without overwriting history.

### AC-E6-505-19 — Support Report Regeneration

**Given** an approved discrepancy resolution requires a corrected report,  
**When** the discrepancy is resolved,  
**Then** the system must make the resolution information available to the authorized report-regeneration process.

### AC-E6-505-20 — Preserve Audit Traceability

**Given** a discrepancy-management event occurs,  
**When** the event is recorded,  
**Then** the system must preserve sufficient audit information to determine who performed the action, what changed, why it changed, when it occurred, and which report and source records were affected.

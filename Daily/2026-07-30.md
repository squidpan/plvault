## REAL ID Docs - paul


```text
passport, social securty card, NJ Driver's license, Joint checking account bank statement, spectrum pseg bill, vzw cell phone bill statement
```
Yes, this specific mix of documents completely satisfies the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission ([NJ MVC](https://www.nj.gov/mvc/)) requirements for a [REAL ID](https://www.nj.gov/mvc/realid/). [1]

By swapping in your PSE&G and Verizon bills, you have actually created an even stronger combination for your address verification. Your paperwork breaks down into the required three steps:

### 1. Proof of Address (2 Documents Required)

Your utility and phone bills cover this step entirely, meaning your NJ Driver's License does not even need to be used as address proof. [1, 2]

- Document 1: Spectrum / PSE&G utility bill (Must be issued within the past 90 days).
- Document 2: Verizon Wireless cell phone bill statement (Must be issued within the past 90 days).
- _Backup Option_: Your NJ Driver's License or the Joint Checking account statement can also serve as address proofs if needed. [1, 2]

### 2. Proof of Social Security Number (1 Document Required)

- Document: Original, unlaminated Social Security Card. [3]

### 3. Identity Points (6 Points Required)

Since your NJ Driver's License is completely freed up from the address step, you can now use it here as an identity document. This combination gives you a comfortable safety cushion: [1, 4]

- Unexpired U.S. Passport: 4 Points (Primary Document).
- NJ Driver's License: 1 Point (Secondary Document).
- Social Security Card: 1 Point (Secondary Document).
- Joint Checking Account Bank Statement: 1 Point (Secondary Document). [5, 6, 7, 8]

Total Point Value: 7 Points (This safely exceeds the 6-point minimum required). Note that while you have three 1-point documents here, the NJ MVC rules state you can only use a maximum of two 1-point documents _towards your final total_. Your passport (4) plus any two of your 1-point documents (2) successfully gets you to exactly 6 points. [6, 9, 10]

### Final Verification Checklist

- 90-Day Rule: Ensure your Spectrum/PSE&G bill and Verizon Wireless bill are printed and dated within 90 days of your appointment.
- 60-Day Rule: If you choose to use the bank statement, it must be dated within the last 60 days.
- Physical Paper: The DMV will not look at your phone. You must bring physical, printed paper copies of your digital statements. [1, 3, 11]

Yes, your documents perfectly satisfy the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission ([NJ MVC](https://www.nj.gov/mvc/)) "2 + 1 + 6 = REAL ID" requirement. [1, 2]

The documents you provided will be categorized to meet the criteria:

### 1. Proof of Address (2 Documents Required)

Your NJ Driver's License serves an entirely separate purpose here than it does for identity points. It fulfills your first address requirement. [2]

- Document 1: Valid NJ Driver's License.
- Document 2: Spectrum utility bill (Must be issued within the past 90 days).

### 2. Proof of Social Security Number (1 Document Required)

- Document: Original, unlaminated Social Security Card. [6]

### 3. Identity Points (6 Points Required)

Because your NJ Driver’s License is being used to fulfill the "Proof of Address" requirement above, you cannot use it again to gain an extra point here. However, your remaining documents still easily reach the 6-point minimum: [7, 8]

- Unexpired U.S. Passport: 4 Points (Primary Document).
- Joint Checking Account Bank Statement: 1 Point (Secondary Document).
- Social Security Card: 1 Point (Secondary Document). [3, 9, 10, 11, 12]

Total Point Value: 6 Points (Fulfills the identity requirement exactly). [1]

### Important Preparation Checklist

- Timeframes: Make sure the Spectrum bill is less than 90 days old and the bank statement is less than 60 days old on the day of your appointment. [2]
- Names: The names on your passport, bank statement, utility bill, and license must match. If you have had a legal name change (e.g., marriage), you must also bring a certified marriage certificate. [13]

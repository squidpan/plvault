# Hello



superman, trey, m3 and johnny brisket,

Just a quick note to say that I still think about each and every one of you.

In my mind, `npqs` is the seminal `streaming` rock album — all songs written, composed, arranged, performed and produced by the band `dev`. That’s you guys.

And me? I was the roadie carrying your instruments.

Lemmy was Hendrix’s roadie way before he fronted Motorhead, and I always dug the idea behind his motto: if you can’t be on stage with the band, then hell, you setup the Marshall amps and the mics, carry their instruments and get as close to the stage as you can.

That’s how I felt working with you guys. I got to hang around some great, seriously talented people, be part of the show, and have a hell of a good time doing it.

Oh, what a trip that was.

I haven't landed anywhere yet. I kept thinking I'd reach out once I had a new job and could report back with some good news, but the search is taking longer than I expected. So I figured, fuck it — I don't need a new job to tell you guys that I miss working with you and hope you're all doing well.

Rock on, brothers!

Paul

# Hello

Hey Tom,

Just a quick note to say that I’m grateful for everything you did over the years to keep me on the payroll — despite my occasionally making that more difficult than it needed to be. :)

I had a great time being part of the team and getting to see what it was like to support the great cloud modernization epic that was `npqs`. I got to experience and see things that are only possible at a place like the Fed, and looking back, those years were really the highlight of my career.

More than anything, though, I know you always looked out for me. I probably didn’t say it enough at the time, but I knew it then and appreciate it even more now.

I’m still looking for my next landing spot. For a while I told myself I’d wait until I found something before checking in — preferably with a nice success story to tell. Well, apparently the job market didn’t get that memo. So rather than wait indefinitely, I wanted to say hello, thank you again for all those years, and see how you and the family are doing.

Hope life is treating you well, Tom. I miss the old gang.

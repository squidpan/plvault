Dear Hiring Manager:

I am writing to express my interest in the Business Analyst position at Proskauer Rose LLP. I bring extensive experience supporting enterprise technology initiatives, with a strong background in requirements analysis, process improvement, workflow documentation, testing, and collaboration between business and technology teams.

Most recently, at the Federal Reserve Bank of New York through Gresham, I supported the multi-year modernization of the streaming market-data platform. This included understanding the existing on-premises Linux and Oracle environment and business workflows—the “as-is” state—and helping translate those requirements into the “to-be” cloud-based environment. I worked with business, product, technology, and infrastructure stakeholders to develop requirements and Jira user stories with acceptance criteria, prioritize functional and non-functional needs, and validate the transition from legacy Oracle-based data access to REST API/JSON-based services.

Throughout my career, I have analyzed business processes, supported UAT, documented workflows, and served as a liaison between technical and non-technical stakeholders. Proskauer's emphasis on as-is/to-be analysis, requirements gathering, process improvement, and translating business requirements into technical requirements therefore aligns particularly well with my experience.

I would welcome the opportunity to bring this experience to Proskauer. Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely,

Paul Lyu

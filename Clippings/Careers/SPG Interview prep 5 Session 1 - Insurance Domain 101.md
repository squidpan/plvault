# Insurance Domain 101 — Session 1

## The Insurance Ecosystem

The insurance industry has several different players. One of the biggest mistakes people make is assuming the insurance company sells directly to customers.

Usually it doesn't.

Instead, there are several organizations involved.

```text
                     INSURANCE ECOSYSTEM

                 Customer / Business Owner
                          │
                          │
                          ▼
                  Retail Agent / Broker
                          │
                    submits business
                          │
                          ▼
                 MGA (Managing General Agent)
          (often using Surefyre as its platform)
                          │
            underwrites within delegated authority
                          │
                          ▼
            Insurance Carrier (Insurance Company)
                          │
               ultimately assumes the risk
                          │
                          ▼
                    Reinsurance Company
           shares part of the carrier's risk
```

# The Players

## 1. Customer (Insured)

The customer wants insurance.

Examples:

- Construction company
    
- Restaurant
    
- Trucking company
    
- Hospital
    
- Law firm
    
- Manufacturer
    

They are purchasing protection against financial loss.

---

## 2. Retail Agent (Producer)

This is usually the customer's primary contact.

The retail agent:

- sells insurance
    
- gathers customer information
    
- explains coverage
    
- completes applications
    
- uploads documents
    
- answers customer questions
    

The retail agent **does not usually make underwriting decisions**.

They are sales and customer relationship professionals.

Examples include:

- Independent insurance agencies
    
- HUB International
    
- Marsh McLennan Agency
    
- Brown & Brown
    
- USI Insurance Services
    
- Lockton
    

Notice something interesting.

**HUB appears in the SPG job description.**

That almost certainly refers to **HUB International**, one of the largest insurance brokerages in North America.

SPG appears to work closely with them.

---

# 3. MGA (Managing General Agent)

This is probably the most important concept for your interview.

An MGA is **not** an insurance company.

An MGA sits between:

Retail Agent

↓

Insurance Carrier

An MGA performs underwriting work on behalf of one or more insurance carriers.

Think of it this way.

The carrier says:

> "You may issue policies for us within these rules."

The MGA therefore has delegated authority.

Typical MGA responsibilities:

- receive submissions
    
- review applications
    
- evaluate risk
    
- request more information
    
- calculate premiums
    
- issue quotes
    
- bind coverage
    
- issue policies
    

Sound familiar?

That's exactly what our diagrams show.

# 4. Insurance Carrier

This is the actual insurance company.

Examples:

- Travelers
    
- Chubb
    
- The Hartford
    
- CNA
    
- Liberty Mutual
    
- Zurich
    
- AXA XL
    
- AIG
    
- Nationwide
    

The carrier:

- provides the insurance capital
    
- assumes financial risk
    
- pays covered claims
    
- establishes underwriting guidelines
    

The MGA is operating within rules established by the carrier.

---

# 5. Reinsurance Company

Insurance companies also buy insurance.

That surprises many people.

Example:

Carrier writes

$500 million

of hurricane exposure.

They don't want to keep all of that risk.

They transfer part of it to:

- Munich Re
    
- Swiss Re
    
- Hannover Re
    
- SCOR
    

This is called **reinsurance**.

You almost certainly will not configure reinsurance in Surefyre, but it's useful context.

# Specialty Insurance

Now the term **Specialty Insurance** makes more sense.

Standard insurance covers common risks.

Examples:

- Personal auto
    
- Homeowners
    
- Small business package
    

Specialty insurance covers unusual or complex risks.

Examples:

|Specialty|Example Customer|
|---|---|
|Cyber|Software company|
|Marine|Cargo ship operator|
|Aviation|Charter airline|
|Professional Liability|Law firm|
|Medical Malpractice|Hospital|
|Environmental|Chemical manufacturer|
|Construction|General contractor|
|Energy|Oil & gas company|
|Inland Marine|Heavy equipment rental|
|Directors & Officers (D&O)|Public company|

These policies usually require more sophisticated underwriting.

That's where platforms like Surefyre become valuable.

# Key Terms to Know

|Term|Meaning|
|---|---|
|**Insured**|Person or company buying insurance|
|**Retail Agent (Producer)**|Sells insurance and works with the customer|
|**Broker**|Represents the customer in obtaining insurance (often used interchangeably with agent in casual discussion, though distinctions can exist)|
|**MGA**|Managing General Agent with delegated underwriting authority from one or more carriers|
|**Carrier**|Insurance company that assumes the financial risk|
|**Reinsurer**|Company that insures insurance companies|
|**Underwriter**|Evaluates risk and determines whether and on what terms to provide coverage|
|**Specialty Insurance**|Insurance for complex or niche risks requiring specialized underwriting|

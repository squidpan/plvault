**Project:**

The Rep Code Expansion initiative is focused on modernizing how advisor and broker identifiers are structured and managed across a financial services platform. The current three character rep code construct is no longer sufficient to support client growth, evolving advisor structures, and increasing operational demands. This project introduces a scalable solution to enable four character rep codes while maintaining compatibility with legacy systems through a centralized translation approach. The effort spans core data models, supervisory workflows, and integrations across trading, clearing, and operational platforms to ensure consistent, enterprise-wide adoption.  
  
Fidelity's current three character rep code structure was designed for legacy needs and is increasingly constrained in a modern financial services environment characterized by advisor growth, M&A activity, and more complex distribution models. Clients are leveraging rep codes for multiple purposes, including identifying individual advisors, teams, compensation structures, and order handling roles, while codes are rarely retired, accelerating capacity exhaustion. Short term mitigation through special characters has proven insufficient, creating poor user experience, limited client adoption, and incompatibility with downstream vendor systems.

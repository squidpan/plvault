## About the job

**Job Title: Administrative - Product Manager**

**Location:** Jersey City, NJ (Hybrid)

**Duration:** 3-12+ Months

**Job Description:**

**Position Overview**

We are seeking an **Administrative - Product Manager I** to support product planning, requirements gathering, and product delivery initiatives. This role requires a candidate who can quickly learn new business processes and product domains, collaborate with stakeholders, and work closely with development teams in an Agile environment. The ideal candidate will have experience translating business needs into user stories and managing work throughout the product development lifecycle.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Gather and document internal business requirements from stakeholders and end users.
- Create and maintain user stories, acceptance criteria, and detailed product documentation.
- Work closely with stakeholders, developers, QA teams, and business partners to ensure requirements are clearly understood and successfully delivered.
- Participate in Agile ceremonies, including sprint planning, backlog refinement, daily stand-ups, reviews, and retrospectives.
- Support product roadmap development, feature prioritization, and product execution activities.
- Manage and track work items using **Jira** and maintain project documentation in **Confluence**.
- Assist with product planning, testing, implementation, and release activities.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure products align with business objectives and customer needs.
- Coordinate pilot programs and gather user feedback to support continuous product improvement.
- Identify opportunities for process optimization and operational efficiency.

**Required Qualifications**

- Bachelor's degree required; advanced degree preferred.
- Experience in Product Management, Business Analysis, Product Ownership, or a related role.
- Strong experience gathering business requirements and creating user stories.
- Experience working in an **Agile/Scrum** environment.
- Hands-on experience with **Jira** and **Confluence**.
- Excellent written, verbal, and interpersonal communication skills.
- Strong analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills.
- Ability to work effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Proven ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
- Must be a **fast learner** and able to adapt quickly due to a steep learning curve.

**Preferred Qualifications**

- Familiarity with AI-powered tools and technologies, including:
- Microsoft Copilot
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Cursor
- Codex
- Rovo
- Experience supporting software or digital product development initiatives.
- Exposure to stakeholder management and cross-functional project coordination.

**Key Skills**

- Product Management

- Agile / Scrum
- Jira
- Confluence
- Stakeholder Management
- Product Roadmap Support
- Business Analysis
- Cross-Functional Collaboration

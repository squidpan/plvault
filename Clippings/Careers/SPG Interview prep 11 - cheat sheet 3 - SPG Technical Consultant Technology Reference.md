# Top 10 Interview Sound Bites

1. **"I see these technologies as tools that support successful implementations rather than isolated technical skills."**
    
2. **"My role is understanding how the business process maps to the technology."**
    
3. **"I'm comfortable reading technical artifacts and collaborating effectively with Engineering."**
    
4. **"I don't need to write every line of code to understand how the solution should behave."**
    
5. **"REST, JSON, and Postman are part of my troubleshooting toolkit."**
    
6. **"JavaScript helps make forms dynamic and responsive to business rules."**
    
7. **"Swagger tells me what an API is supposed to do; Postman helps me verify that it actually does it."**
    
8. **"Python is a practical automation tool for implementations, especially around data transformation and validation."**
    
9. **"Strong testing and production readiness are just as important as successful configuration."**
    
10. **"Technology is only successful when it enables the customer's business process."**

# The One Thing to Remember

If the interviewer points to any technology in the job description and asks:

> **"How would you use this?"**

Think through this sequence:

```text
Customer Requirement

↓

Configuration

↓

Technology

↓

Testing

↓

Deployment

↓

Customer Success
```

That mindset ties every technology back to the work of a Technical Consultant.

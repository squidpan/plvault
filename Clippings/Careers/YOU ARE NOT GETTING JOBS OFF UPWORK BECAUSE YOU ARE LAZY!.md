I agree, this title is very insensitive, but it is the truth. The [last post I made about how I got a gig off Upwork after ten months of consistently searching with no progress](https://medium.com/readers-club/after-10-months-of-searching-for-a-job-i-finally-landed-a-gig-on-upwork-and-i-am-here-to-tell-you-06d69f2360a3) made me catch some flak.

Someone even went as far as sending me horrible emails to tell me how I am misleading people. Because I simply wrote down the things I did that helped me get a job? That is so weird. But that is not why I am writing this post.

I initially started this as a response to that person but I decided against it, I decided instead to write something else, but keep the title, nonetheless. I hope the people who need this find it.

During my years of consistent job hunting, I realized that to make money, you must spend money. As someone who had just graduated the university and only just started adulting for the first time, I could tell you for a fact that I had absolutely no money to spend.

The cycle of wealth making is especially vicious and I say this because you will see your mates from much more well to do families and social backgrounds doing better than you, with less knowledge and expertise than you possess and this…

This is why it is important for you to understand that on this journey to end unemployment and poverty, you cannot compare yourself with others. Matter of fact, the first thing you need to learn is to have mental fortitude, because poverty is humiliating, but not as much as trying to get out of it. The good news however being that, if you do manage to get out of poverty, you will forever be grateful to yourself for not fearing the humiliation it required to breakthrough.

During Covid-19, Beyonce dropped a [video](https://share.google/wfqgeVXMfsnJ0gU6o) for the graduating class and in that video she said something about not worrying about what anyone else is doing and to focus on your own work and I just wanted to put that here because ==true growth is not in doing better than your peers, it is doing better than the old you.==

I cannot make you a list of ways to make money to buy connects on Upwork, or a list on how to make money to run ads, or money to buy a new phone to work with or all the filming equipment you need grow your social media pages, but I can tell you the next best thing. I can tell you how to get ahead faster by simply focusing all your energy into the things within your control.

There are probably a numerous number of reasons why you simply cannot achieve your goals and they are probably all valid, but allow me to tell you the most profound thing I have ever heard in my life:

> If you really wanted something, you would scale the heavens and the earth to get it.

**Here are a few things you can do while you wait for things to align.**

**1.** **Fix that attitude!**

As cliché as this sounds, you cannot go forward if you keep thinking backwards. Really, believe it or not, your attitude has a part to play in whether or not you get a job in the next few months or weeks or even days. Quit the negative self-talk and quiet sabotage. Stop applying to jobs with less effort than it requires. If a job requires you to write a cover letter, draft one! Instead of using shitty ai prompts and submitting ai slop and generic cover letters with no valuable content. Fix that lazy, doubtful, defeated attitude and get to work!

Start with updating your cv and portfolio websites, then go on to create quality samples with a smile on your face and hope in your heart. You can do this!

**2.** **Build capacity while you wait.**

This means that you should not wait for things to become perfect before you start. You want to be a big content creator, but you don’t have a good phone yet? That’s fine, while you wait and save up to buy a new phone, start writing your ideas and mapping them out.

*What type of content do you want to put out? Who is your audience? What are the best types of hooks to use with this particular audience to quickly grab and keep their attention? Who are some of the best influencers and content creators in this field and how do they do it? What types of hooks are working for them, which ones are not?*

Heck you could even go as far as writing scripts for the videos you plan to shoot when you finally get a good phone. If you do due diligence to all (or more realistically, most) of these, while you wait for things to fall in place, when things eventually do fall in place, you will simply hit the ground running! Imagine you get everything you need to create and instead of starting then to learn what to do, you already know what to do and are armed with the experience and discipline required to grow and become one of the best in that field. It puts you miles ahead and makes you about ten times more hireable.

If you are a writer, start planning your newsletters, your weekly features and stories, pick up a pen and a book and simply write that next best seller novel you have had an idea to write for some time now.

**3.** **Ditch the normal way**

So, you have been applying for jobs using the same cover letter format you have been using for months or even years now, the same intro, the same portfolio, the same cv, the same everything. You see, the world is constantly moving and you have to move with it too or you would get behind. Like way behind, and I mean this in the worst possible way.

There are numerous ways to write cover letters now, every online content writer or marketing guru is teaching something on a new way to do the same thing. Basically, what sells nowadays in the job market is no longer just your skillset but also how much you are able to distinguish yourself from others.

A couple of times I have ditched the normal way of doing things, I have ended up landing the gig. I spoke about this more elaborately in my other article on [how I warned $100 on Upwork after tweaking a few things](https://medium.com/@mikaylabarigboma/i-earned-almost-100-off-upwork-in-two-weeks-after-i-did-this-9c06e806227b?sk=5f78d0c95f18c69e6df348fad2ab4d41).

While others are busy telling a client what they are offering, I am busy telling my clients their pain points and offering them free advice and a little hand too if they need one, all for a fee. Repackaging my services and presenting them as if they are free to my clients.

A client says they are looking for a developmental editor; I tell them in my cover letters how to make their stories connect on their own and offer to help if they would rather, I use my expertise to do it. You see, it really is that simple and with the increased outpour and introduction of ai into a lot of professions, you can workup numerous strategies and templates to use to write these things. Again, ditch the normal, think outside of the box. Show your client exactly why they should be hiring you.

**4.** **Bet on yourself.**

I guess if you have read this far then you it must be clear to you that no one is coming to save you. A miracle benefactor will not fall from the sky and start to sponsor all your dreams. No rich auntie or uncle is dying and leaving their wealth or company in your name. You are all by yourself and if you must get out of this, you must decide to do that with sublime dedication. Comit to finishing that ground breaking novel you know would become best seller. Stop procrastinating, set out an hour or two a day to get it done.

Enrol in competitions you feel like you do not qualify for and put in the work required to compete. Even if you fail, you will learn the lesson of discipline and understand the thrill of passion. Do not be afraid to try new things, to branch out, stretch yourself, expand.

You miss a hundred percent of every shot you do not take.

# During the Interview

One thing I hope you'll remember:

You don't have to prove you're the smartest technical person in the room.

You have to convince them that you'd be a good consultant to put in front of one of their customers.

Those are very different goals.

If you don't know something, it's perfectly acceptable to say:

> "I haven't worked with that specific product yet, but based on similar enterprise implementations, here's how I'd approach it..."

I think that answer will earn you more credibility than trying to bluff.

# CHEAT SHEET #1

# SPG Technical Consultant — Insurance Vocabulary

**Interview Goal:**  
Understand enough insurance terminology to have an intelligent conversation with customers and the interviewers. You are **not** interviewing to be an underwriter.

---

# 1. Insurance Ecosystem

```text
Customer (Insured)
        │
        ▼
Retail Agent / Broker
        │
 Submits Application
        │
        ▼
MGA (Managing General Agent)
        │
 Underwrites Risk
        │
        ▼
Insurance Carrier
        │
 Assumes Financial Risk
        │
        ▼
Reinsurance Company
```

### Remember

1. Customer buys insurance.
2. Retail Agent sells insurance.
3. MGA underwrites insurance.

> [!Underwriting]-
> Underwriting in insurance is the process of evaluating the risk of insuring a person or asset to determine eligibility for coverage and the appropriate premium to charge

4. Carrier assumes the financial risk.
5. Surefyre helps manage the workflow between them.

---

# 2. Who Is "The Business"?

When SPG says:

> "The business wants..."

They usually mean:

- Underwriters
- Operations
- Product Owners
- Customer Service
- Compliance
- Professional Services

NOT software developers.

# 3. Key Organizations

## Retail Agent - acts on behalf of customers

- Works with customer
- Collects information
- Submits application
- Answers customer questions
    

Examples

- HUB International
- Marsh McLennan
- Brown & Brown
- Lockton
    

---

## MGA - acts on behalf of carriers

Managing General Agent

***Acts on behalf of insurance carriers.***

Responsibilities

- Receive submissions
- Underwrite
- Request more information
- Quote
- Bind
- Issue policies
    

Think

**Business user of Surefyre.**

---

## Carrier - owns financial risks

Insurance company.

Examples

- Travelers
- Chubb
- AIG
- CNA
- Liberty Mutual
- Zurich
    

Carrier

owns the financial risk.

# My One Suggested Addition

I think every cheat sheet should end with a **"Top 10 Interview Sound Bites"** section. These are short, memorable phrases you can naturally work into your answers.

For this sheet, I'd include:

1. **"Surefyre is a configurable platform, not a custom application."**
    
2. **"The Technical Consultant enables the underwriting process rather than making underwriting decisions."**
    
3. **"MGAs operate with delegated authority from insurance carriers."**
    
4. **"Specialty insurance relies heavily on configurable workflows and business rules."**
    
5. **"Everything before Bind is deciding whether to insure; everything after Bind is servicing the policy."**
    
6. **"The platform supports the business process—the business defines the rules."**
    
7. **"Configuration, testing, and customer adoption are just as important as the technology itself."**
    
8. **"A successful implementation ends with a customer who can confidently operate the solution."**
    
9. **"My experience is in enterprise implementations; the insurance domain is the new part, not the implementation discipline."**
    
10. **"I'm excited to learn Surefyre because the implementation lifecycle closely matches the work I've done throughout my career."**
    

I think adding these "sound bites" to every cheat sheet will make them much more useful the night before and the morning of your interview because they're easy to recall under pressure.

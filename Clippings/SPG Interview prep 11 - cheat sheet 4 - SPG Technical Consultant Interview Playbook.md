# If I Don't Know Something

Never bluff.

Say

> "I haven't worked with that specific technology or product yet, but here's how I'd approach learning it based on similar enterprise implementations I've worked on."

That answer builds trust.

# My Closing Statement

If the interview feels like it's wrapping up:

> "Thank you for the opportunity today. The more I've learned about the role, the more I believe it aligns with the work I enjoy most—understanding business processes, implementing enterprise solutions, working with customers, and helping teams successfully adopt technology. I know there will be a learning curve with Surefyre, but I feel the implementation lifecycle is a strong match with my experience, and I'm excited about the opportunity."

# Friday Morning Review (5 Minutes)

Before you walk into SPG, remind yourself:

✔ I don't need to know everything.

✔ They're hiring an implementation consultant, not a software architect.

✔ My strengths are requirements, testing, troubleshooting, releases, and customer communication.

✔ Be honest about what I'm learning.

✔ Show curiosity.

✔ Speak confidently but accurately.

✔ Connect every answer back to customer success.

- Top 10 REST status codes and methods
    
- Top 10 Postman reminders

Think of it as the one page you could review in **three minutes** while waiting in the lobby.

Not much to report on the employment front. Last week, I had an interview for a contract-to-hire position with a specialty insurance firm down in Berkeley Heights, but it didn't work out. They were really looking for a super-technical Java developer and not a business analyst who has spent his career at the intersection of business and technology, like me.

Yesterday, I applied for a job at the National Hockey League (NHL), just for fun. These days I don't list any of my work experience before 2008, and I got rid of the dates I went to college—1985 and 1989—because I have a feeling they will dismiss my résumé as soon as they realize I went to college before the cell phone era. :)

I am finally meeting Lem, my metal brother from the Netherlands, who is in New York helping out the FRBNY team I used to be part of. He is the one I sent a few Boohwal CDs to, along with _Stolen Paradise_, a dystopian novel about a young woman named Adria who uncovers some very bad things going on inside a supposedly utopian society after an eco-terrorism attack.

Not sure if you've heard of it. :)

I know the author **very well!!!**

Anyway, Lem wanted me to tell you that he read your book and really enjoyed it. I thought you would like to know that your book made its way all the way to the Netherlands and found a fan there.

What about you? Have you read anything good lately?

I love you very much. Talk to you later!
